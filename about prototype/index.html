Nothing Hidden in the Fine Print

In the spaces we design and the way we work

Is this your first venture in real estate?

No. Beyond Concrete was founded on years of design and development work across residential and commercial projects. Ekam is our most considered project to date — but it carries the weight of everything we’ve learned before it.

Are you an architecture studio or a real estate company?

Neither entirely, and both in practice. We design the buildings we develop — which means the same team that draws the section also makes decisions about materials, density, and what gets built at all. That overlap is intentional.

Why are you called Beyond Concrete?

Because buildings are more than their structure. The name is a reminder — to us as much as anyone — that what we build should outlast its materials: in quality, in community, and in the way it sits on the land.

What does IGBC recognition mean for a building?

The Indian Green Building Council evaluates projects across energy use, water efficiency, materials, and indoor air quality. Recognition means a building has met independently verified standards — not just internal claims. Ekam holds a Platinum rating.

How does living in a green building benefit me?

Lower electricity and water bills, better natural light and ventilation, and materials that don't degrade your air quality over time. Green buildings also tend to age better — less maintenance, stronger resale value, and a quieter, more comfortable interior.

Visit our Projects page for detailed documentation on each development — site context, design rationale, sustainability credentials, and availability. You're also welcome to reach out directly; we're happy to walk you through anything in person.
